AACSB (Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business) Accreditation Q&A Depository

Practical questions business-school colleagues have asked about AACSB accreditation, with a summary of what peers answered. Organized by the 2026 AACSB Global Standards; older discussions that cite previous standard numbers are filed under today's standard.

Summaries are written by AELAA from peer discussions in the AACSB Exchange member community (2014–2026), without participants' names. They describe what colleagues reported doing and are not official AACSB guidance, and AELAA is not affiliated with AACSB; always confirm against the current AACSB standards and interpretive guidance.
