About AELAA

Three parts of one job

Teaching, testing and accreditation are usually handled by different offices. AELAA treats them as one craft and connects the educators who practice it.

Learning

Course and program design built around clear outcomes that students can reach and instructors can see.

Assessment

Exams and assignments that measure those outcomes fairly, with the evidence to show which questions work.

Accreditation

Turning everyday assessment results into the documentation accreditors ask for, without a separate scramble each cycle.

Member benefit

The AELAA Grader

Grade bubble-sheet exams with the printer and scanner you already have, and see which questions are doing their job.

  1. Enter your answer key for up to 100 questions, including questions with more than one right answer, bonus and dropped questions.
  2. Print bubble sheets sized to your exam, blank or with each student's name and ID.
  3. Scan and grade. Upload scanned PDFs or phone photos; every sheet is read and scored in seconds.
  4. Check every question with difficulty, discrimination, distractor analysis and KR-20 reliability.
Open the AELAA Grader

Free for AELAA members. Sign in with the email on your membership. Grading happens in your browser, so student data never leaves your computer.

Membership

Join AELAA

Membership is open to educators, assessment staff and accreditation coordinators. Members receive:

  • Full access to the AELAA Grader and AELAA Oral Exams
  • The AACSB (Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business) Accreditation Q&A Depository: nearly 500 practical accreditation questions with peers' answers, organized by AACSB standard
  • A community of colleagues working on the same problems

Membership is $75 a year, paid securely through Zeffy. After you pay, create your account on the Grader with the same email address you used on Zeffy. Access starts within a few minutes and lasts one year. Renewing early adds a year to your current end date.
